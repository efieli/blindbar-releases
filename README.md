# Blindbar

The Touch Bar on my MacBook Pro stopped lighting up, but it still responds to touch. Instead of giving up on it, I built Blindbar.

Blindbar shows the Touch Bar on your screen. As you slide your finger along the real one, you can follow where you are on the on-screen version and only tap once you're on the right button. It's a small fix, but it means I can keep using the Touch Bar every day without being able to see it.

If your Touch Bar display died but the touch still works, Blindbar might help you too.
